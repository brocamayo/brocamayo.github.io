Privacy Policy for socialq

Last updated: September 30, 2026

socialq is a personal tool that Broc Mayo uses to schedule and publish his own videos to his own YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and X accounts. It is not offered to the public. It has no website where you sign up, and it does not collect information about anyone else.

What socialq accesses

When an account owner logs in, socialq receives permission to upload videos and publish posts on that account. It uses that permission only to:

  • upload videos the account owner has chosen and queued;
  • set the title, description, caption, hashtags and thumbnail the account owner has written;
  • check that the upload finished processing, and read back the link to the published post.

socialq does not read your viewers', followers' or subscribers' data, your messages, your comments, or your analytics.

Where data is stored

socialq runs on the account owner's own computer. Login tokens, the posting schedule, and a log of what was published are stored only in files on that computer. socialq has no server and no database, and it does not send your data anywhere except to the platform you are posting to.

Sharing

socialq does not sell, rent or share data with anyone. Data is only sent to Google/YouTube, Meta/Instagram, TikTok or X, and only to publish the content you asked it to publish.

YouTube API Services

socialq uses YouTube API Services to upload videos. By using socialq with a YouTube account, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

socialq's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

You can revoke socialq's access at any time:

  • Google / YouTube: Google Account → Third-party connections
  • Instagram / Facebook: Facebook Settings → Business Integrations
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions
  • X: Settings → Security and account access → Apps and sessions

To delete all stored data, delete the socialq credentials folder and state.json file from the computer it runs on. No copies are kept anywhere else.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions can be sent to Broc Mayo on LinkedIn or X.
